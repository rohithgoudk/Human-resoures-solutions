Our Story

A quiet obsession with the details

What began as two former People leads fixing broken spreadsheets for a handful of startup friends has grown into a platform trusted by 500+ companies across 40+ countries. Every policy, every payroll run and every hire is still handled the same way — by a specialist who knows your company by name.

2011
Stackly founded in Austin by two former People leads tired of duct-taped HR.
2015
Expanded into full payroll and compliance coverage across 12 US states.
2019
Reached 250 client companies and opened our remote specialist bench.
2024
Named HR Partner of the Year by two industry associations.

Our Mission

To give growing companies enterprise-grade people operations, without the enterprise overhead — payroll, hiring and benefits run by specialists who treat your team like their own.

Our Vision

To become the standard for how mid-market companies run HR — precise, human and refreshingly simple, no matter how fast the headcount grows.
